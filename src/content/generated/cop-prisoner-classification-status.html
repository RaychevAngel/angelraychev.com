Within the main interval, 2,248 gaps have m < 3n, extending through order 103; 3,893 have m ≥ 3n, all at orders at most 42. Every 3n ≤ m ≤ n(n − 3) is settled with explicit constructions for n ≥ 43. The full interval starting at 2n is settled for n ≥ 104.

This is the complement of the explicitly indexed proved domains and certified witnesses. It does not assume that a failed construction is impossible, or that every further consequence of the theory has already been extracted. Explicit constructions are a separate requirement

The objective requires concrete adjacency rules or a specified composition, with parameters determined from (n,m). Generic search or an existence proof is insufficient; deterministic derandomization does not automatically complete this requirement. Certified listed finite exceptions are accepted.

The separate audit found 0 known-value construction-only gaps within the currently indexed coverage. Every n ≥ 104, 22 ≤ m ≤ n(n − 1) already has an explicit recipe: the main interval uses rings, protected and five-jump circles, layered hubs, antichain codes and dense blocks, without derandomization; sparse budgets and the staircase have their own direct rules and fixed finite exceptions. At smaller orders, all exact providers have direct recipes or saved certified graphs. Omitting the orientation derandomization rule changes no recorded bound.

This conclusion comes from auditing the actual recipes. The numerical reduction to n ≤ 103 alone would not establish it. A future existential equality would leave an explicit-construction obligation, possibly over an infinite domain. Closing small numerical cases alone is not the stopping condition: each pair must have both its value proof and its explicit optimal graph.

The protected-routing and orientation theorems retain their conditional-expectation procedures and stronger routing guarantees. Direct alternative graphs at the same resources are not claimed to preserve all those extra properties. Specified greedy residue rules, cyclic-code listing and fixed parameter tables already give concrete recipes; they are not relabelled existential merely because their discovery involved computation.

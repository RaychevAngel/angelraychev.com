The years after the [2022 track season](/running/record/2022/) were uneven.
Running continued: long outings in 2023, interval work in early 2024, the Orange
County Marathon that May and more long runs in the autumn. The large recorded
gap is 2025. This history is part of what I bring to the
[2026 return](/running/record/2026/), even though it was not one continuous
middle-distance programme.

## April–May: longer night runs and Orange County

April's route recordings show outings growing from roughly seven kilometres to
twenty-five. The longer ones include [**20.12 km in 1:30:09 on 19 April**](https://www.strava.com/activities/11215182018),
about **4:29/km**, [**22.50 km in 1:50:35 on 23 April**](https://www.strava.com/activities/11244583014),
about **4:55/km**, and [**24.64 km in 1:53:55 on 24 April**](https://www.strava.com/activities/11260146860),
about **4:37/km**. Those times and paces are moving summaries,
with exported elevation-gain estimates around **58–72 m**. These are local start dates, established
from the recordings; the last appears as 25 April in the UTC export.

They give the marathon surrounding context. They do not recover a complete
marathon plan or prove how much unrecorded training I did.

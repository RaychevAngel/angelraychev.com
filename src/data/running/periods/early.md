**2000 m has a special place in my running history.** In sixth grade, I ran a road time trial in **8:33**, about **4:17/km** over the remembered roughly 2 km distance. There was a roughly 2 km loop in my neighbourhood that I kept returning to over the years; I estimate I ran around a thousand kilometres on it. I no longer live there, but it helps explain why I chose 2000 m for the first time trial of my present return.

That childhood 8:33 is a recollection, without an exact date. It is separate from two organized 2 km races whose results I have now recovered. The older race record also reaches back several years before my first recorded Strava run.

<h2 id="15-october-2017--the-sofia-short-race">15 October 2017: Sofia Marathon, short distance</h2>

I was **fourteen**, in **eighth grade**, when I ran the Sofia Marathon's short-distance race. The [official result](https://marathonsofia.com/results/2017/sofia2017_10km_overall.pdf) lists **10.5 km**, **49:04 chip time**, **49:12 gun time**, and **61st among the men**, under **Angel RAYCHEV**, bib **2164**. The chip time corresponds to about **4:40/km** using that published distance label.

I remember this as a very hard race, with the start near Orlov most. The [organizer's contemporary instructions](https://sofia2018.bg/2017/10/10/%D0%BF%D1%80%D0%BE%D0%B3%D0%BD%D0%BE%D0%B7%D0%B8%D1%80%D0%B0%D1%82-%D1%80%D0%B5%D0%BA%D0%BE%D1%80%D0%B4%D0%B8-%D0%BD%D0%B0-wizz-air-%D1%81%D0%BE%D1%84%D0%B8%D1%8F-%D0%BC/) place the start in front of Vasil Levski National Stadium and describe one short lap of an older four-lap marathon course.

That is the older layout behind my memory. The later races I ran in 2020 and 2021 used a different marathon layout, with the start at Battenberg Square. The shared [race-results record](/running/record/results/) keeps their course information together.

## 2018–2019: the recorded history begins

The first run in my exported Strava history is from **June 2018**. The export contains **ten run uploads from 2018** and **eight from 2019**. The ledger below preserves each of them. Their descriptions are blank, so there is much less context than in the later interval records.

A few titles still tell me what the run was for. The [20 June 2019](https://www.strava.com/activities/2464740088) and [22 June](https://www.strava.com/activities/2469773573) entries say **“Running uphill, walking downhill.”** Their exported elevation-gain estimates are about **184 m and 240 m** over 8.00 and 8.21 recorded kilometres. The whole-recording paces include the walking descents; they do not isolate the uphill running. **“Helping my friend with his running session”** is another title, alongside ordinary morning and evening runs.

These are the beginnings of the recorded history, rather than the beginnings of running itself. The neighbourhood loop and the earlier organized races already existed. The fuller [2020 record](/running/record/2020/) and [2021 season](/running/record/2021/) follow from here.

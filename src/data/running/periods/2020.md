The summer records centre on two and five kilometres. By September I was mixing
paces on the NSA track. In November, many recorded outings were eight to ten
kilometres; December includes a twenty-kilometre run. The year connects the
[childhood park races](/running/record/early/) with the more deliberate,
repeated workouts of [2021](/running/record/2021/).

## September: changing pace on the NSA track

I recorded these sessions as laps of a nominal **300 m** small track. The faster
blocks alternated with slower running, rather than standing rests. The historical
labels were tempo, endurance and VO₂max; they describe how I classified the work,
not measured oxygen uptake or lactate turnover.

The listed lap counts correspond to nominal totals of **3.6, 5.4, 5.4 and 6.3 km**.
The three 5K claims therefore concern portions of longer mixed-pace sessions.
Their exact boundaries and timing method have not been recovered.

**Distance qualification.** A March 2021 post in my export questions whether
this small track was really 300 m per lap, or closer to
293–294 m; a later comment suggested 295 m. Neither establishes a corrected length. I retain
the nominal distances I used at the time; these sessions should not be treated
as verified 5000 m races. The independently recorded
[South Park 18:16](/running/record/2021/#10-april--south-park-5-km) remains my
5 km race personal best.

The end of September was interrupted. A
[28 September entry](https://www.strava.com/activities/4124275823) is titled
“First run after a small injury.” It records the return without preserving a
diagnosis or the exact beginning of the interruption.

## 11 October — the Sofia short-distance race

**44:27**, the official gun and chip time, aged **17**. The event's results list
the short race as **10 km**, with **25th in the men's classification**, bib 3068.
My activity was titled “Sofia marathon 31/1042”; that title's placing should not
replace the organizer's men's classification.

The contemporary activity description reports repeated stops because of stomach
and chest pain. Looking back, I remembered following the leading woman, dropping
away when my stomach hurt, then finding enough strength for a strong final
sprint. The memory and the activity note describe a difficult race; neither
changes the official time.

I ran the same remembered route again in
[2021](/running/record/2021/#10-october--the-sofia-short-distance-race).
Those editions started around Battenberg Square on the yellow paving. My earlier
Orlov Most start belongs to the [2017 race](/running/record/early/#15-october-2017--the-sofia-short-race).

The short-course labels differ between editions: 10 km in 2020, 10.5 km in 2021.
The full and half marathons' certification does not establish the exact length
of the separate short course. I keep the organizer's edition-specific label,
without turning the comparison into a certified 10,000 m personal best.

Sources: [official 2020 results](https://my.raceresult.com/160489/results/pdf?name=02-Results%7C10%20-%20Finisher%20List&contest=3&lang=en)
and [my activity](https://www.strava.com/activities/4179214052).

December's entries include **15.00 km on the 4th**, **20.00 km on the 5th** and
**16.01 km on the 16th**. These distances describe recorded outings, not measured
courses. The month also contains very easy running and a note about feeling
extremely bad; it was not a uniform sequence of hard sessions.

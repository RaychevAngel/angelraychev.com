This is the running programme recovered from my old training workbook.
The calendar was reconstructed on 9 October 2026; the sheet's week numbers
and weekday columns did not themselves contain dates. The completed season,
races and selected workouts are in [2022: a track season](/running/record/2022/).

## Matching the sheet to the season

The sixteen-week training sheet has weekday columns and week numbers, but no
calendar dates. Six matching dated sessions imply the same origin:
**Week 1 was 7–13 March 2022**.

| Week | Date | Matching record |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 3 | [26 March](https://www.strava.com/activities/6886754696) | 4×300 / 3 min + 4×200 / 90 s |
| 4 | [2 April](https://www.strava.com/activities/6918856301) | The same distances, sets and recoveries |
| 5 | [9 April](https://www.strava.com/activities/6952879259) | 800 m maximum effort → 2:12 time trial |
| 10 | [14 May](https://www.strava.com/activities/7140332978) | 1500 m maximum effort → 4:29.67 time trial |
| 11 | [18 May](https://www.strava.com/activities/7162862560) | 600 m maximum effort → 1:31.49 time trial |
| 11 | [21 May](https://www.bfla.org/2022/2022/0_drugi/05_21_new_stars.pdf) | 1000 m maximum effort → 2:42.79 race |

The 12 March record adds a close match with a distance variation: the sheet
prescribes 200s, while I recorded 185s with the same set and recovery pattern.
The calendar is therefore strongly inferred; the original sheet itself does
not date Week 1.

The programme and completed records differ elsewhere too. The
[7 March activity](https://www.strava.com/activities/6786827024) describes
approximately **5×1080 m / 5 min**, against planned 1000s. The sheet has
**4×150 m / 6 min** on 6 May; the
[completed activity](https://www.strava.com/activities/7095990088) says
**eight-minute** rests. On 9 May the plan says **2000 + 1600**; the
[record](https://www.strava.com/activities/7117231269) says **2000 + 1200**.
A dated plan helps recover intentions, but it cannot fill missing details in
what I actually did. Even a matching format on the same date does not independently
confirm a recovery that appears only in the planned cell.

The sheet also contains eight *Injured* annotations, mapped to 15–25 March
under this calendar. It supplies no diagnosis. Filled training entries continue
through **Week 12, 23–29 May**. Weeks 13–15 have only Sunday rest entries,
and Week 16 is unfilled. The later time trials, workouts and championship races
show why a blank cell must remain an unfilled cell, rather than become a rest day.

The June 800 race falls on a Sunday still marked *REST*. The 22 June 600 + 400
falls in blank Week 16; its recovery cannot be taken from the similar planned
**600 / 10 min / 400 on 25 May**. The 27 June workout and July championships
are beyond the sheet's sixteen-week span.

Below is the complete running sheet on the inferred calendar. Its original
notation is retained: distances are in metres, an apostrophe marks minutes and
a double quote marks seconds. *Blank* means no entry. *REST*, *Strength*,
*Injured* and *Only warm-up* remain distinct entries. None of these cells alone
proves completion. The workbook's separate armwrestling sheet has no established
calendar and is not included here.
